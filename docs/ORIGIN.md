# Origin

## The initial impulse

Cognitive Lenses began from a desire to interact with **ideas as living perspectives**, rather than only with AI personas modeled after fixed individuals.

The project asks whether a bounded body of text can be distilled into a reusable pattern of attention:

- what becomes salient;
- what distinctions appear;
- what mechanisms become plausible;
- what questions emerge next.

This is not mind reconstruction.

It is not voice cloning.

It is not doctrinal authority.

It is a source-scoped engineering attempt to recover a **way of seeing** from text and make that perspective conversational.

## The stage-and-lens metaphor

Keep the analytical object fixed.

Change the lens.

Observe what changes in:
- salience;
- framing;
- causality;
- evidence;
- questions.

The stage stays still.

The lens changes.

That tension is the central play mechanic of the project.
